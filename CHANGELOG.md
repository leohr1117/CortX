# Changelog

## 1.2.0

Notes show light formatting while you read them. A line that starts with "- " becomes a list, "[]" and "[x]" become checkboxes, and text between double asterisks is bold. This shows on a topic, in Inbox, and in search results. The saved text stays plain. Capture and edit stay text fields.

## 1.1.0

Search your notes from the library. The field looks through every topic and Inbox. Each match shows the note and its topic, newest first. Open a match to read that note where it was filed.

## 1.0.0

The first release of Cortex. Write a note and move on. The app files it for you.

Sign in with your Stride account. Your notes stay with that account. Change your password in Stride, and sign out from the library. A draft stays on the device while you are offline, and notes you file offline sync after you sign in.

File from the capture screen with File or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter. A #topic picks the topic. Without one, the note goes to Inbox. Change the topic right after filing. The library lists your topics. Rename, merge, or delete a topic, and undo. Edit or delete a note, and undo.
